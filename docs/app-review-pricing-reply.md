Thank you for your feedback. We have revised the subscription screens to clarify the full recurring charge and billing period, with introductory offers and calculated price comparisons presented separately.

You can access the available paywall variants using these steps:

1. Complete onboarding and open Profile → Settings.
2. Under Preferences, select “Azo’s Secret Layer.”
3. Enter the password: **azo-review**
4. Select an offering, then choose a paywall layout.
5. Select a subscription and tap the purchase button to open Apple’s purchase sheet.

The “Welcome gift” and “Price drop” entries provide access to the exit offers.

These screens use live RevenueCat offerings, App Store product prices, and the app’s purchase and restore functionality. Free and paid introductory offers appear only when the reviewing Apple account is eligible; otherwise, regular subscription pricing is displayed.
