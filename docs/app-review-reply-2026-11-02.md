## Reply

Hello,

Thank you for the review. Here are the steps to locate the In-App Purchases:

1. Launch Azora and complete onboarding by answering the questions (about 2–3 minutes).
2. After the personalized plan preview, the subscription screen appears. Tap Continue through the short intro screens to reach the plan selection.
3. The subscriptions are listed there with their title, length and billed price. Select one and tap the purchase button to start the StoreKit purchase sheet.
4. After onboarding, the same subscriptions are also available from Profile → Upgrade to Pro.

**Access restrictions:** None by storefront or device. Plans are served through RevenueCat offerings, and each user sees one offering of two plans. For this review, all placements are set to a single offering that contains exactly the subscriptions in this submission. Other subscriptions have been removed from this submission and will be submitted separately.

**Introductory offers:** Free and paid trials are shown only when StoreKit reports the account as eligible. If the sandbox Apple ID has already used an introductory offer for this subscription group, the subscription is shown at its regular price. A fresh sandbox account will show the trial.

The Paid Apps Agreement has been accepted and is active. The purchase screen includes working links to our Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy. A screen recording of the full flow is attached.
