# Liven: AI Life Coach — App Store review research

Research date: **October 11, 2026**. App ID: `6450840109`. Storefront: **United States**.

For specific feature examples and review citations, see [Liven feature requests](liven-feature-requests.md).

## Features people value

The clearest theme is **help understanding feelings and choosing a manageable next step**. The companion receives especially specific praise. The following order reflects qualitative evidence, not counted feature votes.

| Feature | What reviewers value | Supporting reviews |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Supportive AI companion** | Venting without judgment, emotional validation, follow-up questions, and practical coping guidance. | AmyL-M `14507554057` ([P1][p1]); Mamamorty `14239405991` ([P6][p6]); First time AI chat `14164394720` ([P9][p9]). |
| **Guided journaling and quick check-ins** | Prompts that uncover feelings; short dictated entries make reflection easier to fit into a day. | cindecker `14181740635` ([P8][p8]); ManorChick `14185750454` ([P7][p7]). |

**For Azora:** A flow worth exploring is **quick check-in → relevant coping exercise → optional reflection → saved progress**. This is a proposed product direction, not a requested implementation. Breathing has concrete positive examples here; the sample does not establish how many customers want it.

## Scope and method

Read the ten reviews displayed on the [requested iPhone reviews page](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/liven-ai-life-coach/id6450840109?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone) and **500 unique written reviews** returned by Apple's public US customer-review feed, pages 1–10, sorted by most recent update. Every feed page contained 50 reviews; no review IDs were duplicated. The final page's next link points back to page 10.

The feed's review update dates span **June 6–October 9, 2026**. This is an accessible recent-review window, not every historical review. The feed does not identify the reviewer's device, so these cannot all be confirmed as iPhone reviews. Star-only ratings and developer responses are absent from the feed. An update date can reflect an edited review.

This document contains **paraphrased review summaries and qualitative findings**, rather than a verbatim archive. Specific praise supports the feature findings; generic praise and star ratings alone do not. Feature categories overlap, and their ordering is an interpretation of the evidence, not a measured demand ranking. Complaints and reported benefits are reviewers' accounts, not independently verified facts.

Two displayed reviews, by Jcg324 and Mamamorty, have matching text in the feed. The displayed-page sample and feed sample are therefore not added together.

## What weakens the experience

| Issue | Evidence and implication |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscription confusion** | Match Data Corrupt reports billing after cancellation, including confusion about app and website subscriptions; Lopophoto and Mop28 report related problems. | **Interruptions and rating pressure** | Dumplindidit, Fonseneca, Imnotsuremz, and LRiley82! describe repeated upsells or rating requests. Feedback collected before meaningful use offers weak evidence of sustained value. ([P1][p1], [P2][p2], [P5][p5], [P10][p10]) |

## Summary of learnings

The strongest opportunity suggested by this research is a low-effort path from emotional reflection to a useful action. For product discovery, test whether people return after their first week, whether a coping session helps them choose what to do next, and whether saved reflections remain useful. These are proposed validation questions, not outcomes demonstrated by the reviews. Avoid treating first-day enthusiasm as evidence of lasting benefit.
